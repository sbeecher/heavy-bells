Heavy Bells

A small museum of dull rules that make lovely things, and the boring reasons they exist.

Every exhibit is a toy you can play with, plus the true, slightly boring story of why the rule had to exist. Usually something was too heavy, too slow, or too dirty.
  • Coin-flip tiles

    A priest finds a stack of identical tiles for a chapel floor. The only choice is which way to turn each one. (Truchet, 1704.)

  • Even Beats

    A timing rule for a particle accelerator turns out to be the tresillo.

  • Heavy Bells

    Church bells are too heavy to move more than one place, so ringers made a rule.

  • Ghost Strums

    A guitarist's arm never stops. The swings that miss the strings keep the time.

  • Only One Switch

    Switches never flip at quite the same moment, so a dial must count in an order where only one changes at a time.

  • Keeps Its Shape

    Cut a sheet in half and it should keep its shape. Only one ratio obeys, which is why your printer paper is 210 by 297.
