Coin-flip tiles

Type anything. Your words become a seed, the seed flips 256 coins, and each coin turns one tile one way or the other.

Same words, same pattern, every time. Change one letter and the whole picture reshuffles.

The rule: every square holds the same tile, two quarter-circles in opposite corners. Flip a coin to decide which way it faces. Nothing else.

Not sure Who first drew the quarter-circle tile. Cyril Stanley Smith wrote about it in 1987 and made it famous, but Wikipedia says the board games Trax and the Black Path Game used the same tile before him. It gives no dates, and I haven't found the first.

Sébastien Truchet catalogued these tile combinations in 1704. Cyril Stanley Smith made the quarter-circle version famous in 1987 (Wikipedia, Truchet tiles).
