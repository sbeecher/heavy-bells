Heavy Bells

Change-ringing bells are heavy and swing through almost a full circle, so a ringer can only nudge a bell a little earlier or later. That gives a dull rule: each change, a bell may move at most one place. Add a second rule, never ring the same order twice, and out comes the sound English towers have made since the 1600s.

Each column is one change: the order the bells strike, top to bottom. The blue line follows one bell as it "hunts" from front to back and back again. Every column in a plain course is different, and the last one is back in rounds.
