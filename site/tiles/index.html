Coin-flip tiles

Type anything. Your words become a seed, the seed flips 256 coins, and each coin turns one tile one way or the other. Sébastien Truchet catalogued these tile combinations in 1704. The quarter-circle version is Cyril Stanley Smith's, from 1987.
