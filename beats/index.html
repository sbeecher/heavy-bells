Even Beats

Spread k beats as evenly as you can over n steps. A rule written to time pulses in a neutron accelerator turns out to make rhythms people have danced to for centuries.

Names and patterns from Godfried Toussaint, “The Euclidean Algorithm Generates Traditional Musical Rhythms” (2005), cgm.cs.mcgill.ca. The algorithm is E. Bjorklund’s (Los Alamos, 2003), written for timing gates in spallation neutron source accelerators. Some rhythms are usually played starting from a different beat (a rotation); the paper notes this for the bossa nova.
